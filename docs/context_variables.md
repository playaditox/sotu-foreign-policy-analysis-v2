# Available context and planned force comparison

The dated context layer shown in the report includes presidential party and administration, systemic era, bounded crisis/campaign context, exact-date president–Congress alignment, term stage, election timing, document format and original length. Counts retain 26 unified Democratic, 11 unified Republican, 47 opposition-both-chambers and 16 split-chamber documents. Metadata describe observed settings; the plots have not held every field constant.

Force advocacy, conditional force and restraint are distinct from the four traditions. They can coexist. The two source-linked illustrations shown in the report explain those distinctions; corpus-wide force outcomes and party/alignment effects have not been estimated. No stated position is not dovishness, and unresolved judgments are not zeros.

Economic conditions, approval and chamber margins remain potential extensions, subject to pre-speech timing, coverage and source checks. The [public release plan](release_status.md) describes those candidates and the separate planned presidential UN General Assembly corpus.
